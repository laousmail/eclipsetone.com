Questions, answered.

Who owns the song?

You do. It comes out under your name, and once the project is paid in full, the song, the instrumental we made and your vocal recordings are yours, in writing. You keep 100% of your songwriting. A cover still belongs to its writers, so it needs a licence. We may share your song on our channels, credited to EclipseTone for the production.

What do I need to send?

A voice memo, your lyrics or a demo, plus one or two songs you like the sound of. Just an idea? We write it with you. Your final vocals are recorded later, in our studio or at one near you.

How fast is it?

Usually, you hear the first version of the music within 7 days, and the finished song takes 2 to 3 weeks, depending on your recording date and your changes. 2 rounds of changes on the music and 2 on the mix are included.

How does payment work?

Nothing to pay to start your project. Once you approve your price: half to start, half once you approve the music, before your vocal session. Final files are sent once everything is paid.

Which currency? Are taxes included?

Prices are in Canadian dollars. The Prices page can show an estimate in US dollars or euros, and your bank converts when you pay. Clients in Canada pay GST and QST on top. Clients outside Canada don’t.
