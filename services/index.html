FAQ

Who owns the music?

You do. 100%. Once it’s paid, the final song, the beat and your vocals are yours, in writing. Individual instrument tracks and project files aren’t included.

Who gets the credit?

You release the song under your name. We may share it on EclipseTone’s channels and with labels we work with, credited to the team of EclipseTone for the production.

Which currency? Are taxes included?

All prices are in Canadian dollars. You can view an estimate in US dollars or euros, and your bank converts when you pay. For clients in Canada, GST and QST are added on top. Clients outside Canada pay no Canadian sales tax.

How does payment work?

Half to start, half before you get the final files. Drafts are shared as previews during the project.

How long does it take?

First version 7 days after we agree on the plan. Finished song in 2 to 3 weeks, depending on your changes.

How many changes can I ask for?

2 rounds on the music and 2 on the mix. Most artists don’t need them all. More rounds can be added for a set price.

What if I already wrote the song?

Pick Full Song. We go straight from your vocals or demo to a finished song.

Do you use AI?

No, and we never will. Every note is written, played and mixed by people on our team.

Where are you based?

Montréal. We work with artists anywhere, fully online.
