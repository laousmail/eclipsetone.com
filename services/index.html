FAQ

Who owns the music?

You do, once the project is paid in full: the final song, the instrumental we made and your vocal recordings are yours, in writing, and you keep 100% of your songwriting. A cover still belongs to its writers, so you need a licence to release it. Separate instrument tracks and project files aren’t included.

Who gets the credit?

You release the song under your name. We may share it on EclipseTone’s channels, credited to EclipseTone for the production. Tell us if you’d rather we waited until your release date.

Which currency? Are taxes included?

All prices are in Canadian dollars. You can view an estimate in US dollars or euros, and your bank converts when you pay. For clients in Canada, GST and QST are added on top. Clients outside Canada pay no Canadian sales tax.

How does payment work?

Half to start, half once you approve the music, before your vocal session. Final files are sent once everything is paid. Drafts are shared as previews during the project.

How long does it take?

The first version of the music is usually ready within 7 days after we agree on the plan and have your memo. The finished song usually takes 2 to 3 weeks, depending on your recording date and your changes. These are estimates, not guarantees.

How many changes can I ask for?

2 rounds on the music and 2 on the mix. A round is one list of changes sent together. Extra rounds are priced before we do them, and only if you say yes.

What if I already wrote the song?

Pick Full Song. We build the music around your song, record your vocals and finish it.

Do you use AI?

We don’t use AI to generate music or vocals. Every part is played, recorded and mixed by people.

Where are you based?

Montréal. Vocals are recorded in our Montréal studio, or at a studio near you with our guide. Everything else works online, wherever you are.
