2. Your quote is the contract

Before you pay anything, we email you a written quote with what we will make, the price of each item, taxes, the total in Canadian dollars, the expected dates, the stages of the work and these terms. The project starts when you accept the quote in writing and pay the first stage. We email you a copy of the accepted quote and these terms right after you accept, and always within 15 days.

3. Paying in stages

Prices are in Canadian dollars. Clients in Canada pay GST and QST on top; clients outside Canada don’t. You pay in two stages, each one before the work it pays for begins:

  • Stage 1, half the price, to start. It pays for the arrangement, the musicians and the production up to the first version of the music.
  • Stage 2, the other half, once you approve the music and before your vocal session. It pays for the vocal recording, the changes, the mix, the master and the delivery. If you record your vocals somewhere else, stage 2 is due before we start the mix.

For a service other than a song (mixing, a video, a website, physical copies), your quote says what each stage covers and when it is due. We accept the payment methods listed in your quote. During the project you hear every version as a preview; the final files are sent once the project is paid in full.

4. Recording your vocals

  • Full Song includes one 2-hour vocal session in our Montréal studio, booked together once you approve the music. Extra studio time is $50 per hour, confirmed before we use it.
  • To move a session, tell us at least 48 hours before. With less notice, or if you don’t come, the session counts as used, because that studio time was held for you. A new session is then $50 per hour.
  • Recording somewhere else? We send you a recording guide, and the cost of that studio is yours. We check your takes before the mix and tell you if anything needs to be recorded again.
  • Your voice memo or demo is a guide for building the music. It is not used as the final vocal unless we both agree in writing.

5. Dates and changes

Your quote gives the expected delivery date. It depends on you sending what we need on time and on your recording date: time spent waiting for your feedback, files, payment or recording date moves it by the same amount, and we confirm any new date with you in writing (a WhatsApp message counts). The first version of the music is usually ready within 7 days of receiving your memo, and a Full Song usually takes 2 to 3 weeks in all. Your package includes a set number of rounds of changes (for example, 2 on the music and 2 on the mix). A round is one list of changes sent together. Extra rounds, or changes to a part you already approved, are priced before we do them, and we only do them if you say yes.

6. If you go quiet

If we don’t hear from you for 60 days after we ask for your feedback, your files or a recording date, we close the project. You pay only for the work done up to then, as set out in section 8. You can reopen it later when a spot is free.

7. Your music and your rights

  • You confirm you own, or have permission to use, everything you send us: lyrics, melodies, vocals, recordings, photos and logos.
  • Once the project is paid in full, the final song, the instrumental we made for it and your vocal recordings are yours, in writing. Until then, we keep the rights to our work.
  • Your songwriting stays yours, 100%, including the lyrics and melodies we help you write in Song + Songwriting. We claim no writing credit or songwriting royalties.
  • Separate instrument tracks and project files aren’t included unless your quote says so.
  • Covers: a song written by someone else needs a licence before you release it. Most distributors arrange it for you. Getting it is your responsibility; we can show you how.
  • Samples: we only use samples that are cleared or that you have the right to use, and we tell you which ones are in your song.
  • We may share the finished work on our channels, credited to EclipseTone for the production. If you’d rather we didn’t, or want us to wait until your release date, tell us in writing.

8. Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel your project at any time. Tell us in writing, by WhatsApp or email. What you get back depends on how far the work has gone, and we always refund within 15 days of your message.

  • Before we start: we refund everything you paid. We start within 2 business days after a payment, unless your quote gives another date.
  • After we start: you pay for the work done up to your message, at the prices in your quote, plus the costs we already paid for your project and can’t get back (for example a session musician, studio time or a factory order). We refund the rest of what you paid. We never charge you for work we haven’t done.
  • How the work is counted: stage 1 is fully earned once the first version of the music is delivered. A vocal session counts as work done once it has taken place, or once it is missed or moved with less than 48 hours’ notice (section 4).
  • Your files: you receive the files made up to your message once that work is paid. The rights in section 7 come with full payment of the project.
  • CDs, USB-C cards and cassettes: once you approve the proof and we order them from the factory, that order can’t be cancelled or refunded. If they arrive damaged, or with a mistake that wasn’t in the proof you approved, we replace them.
  • Taste isn’t a defect. If the work matches your quote and what you approved, we don’t refund it because you changed your mind about the sound; that’s what the rounds of changes are for.
  • If we can’t finish your project, we refund everything you paid for the parts we didn’t deliver.

Your rights for a contract made at a distance

Because we agree on your project by email or message, Québec’s Consumer Protection Act also lets you cancel and get everything you paid back in these cases:

  • within 7 days after you receive your copy of the contract, if we didn’t give you the information the law requires before you accepted, or if your copy is missing it or isn’t easy to keep and print;
  • within 30 days after you accept, if we don’t send you your copy of the contract within 15 days;
  • at any time before you receive it, if your project isn’t delivered within 30 days after the date in your quote (or a new date we agreed in writing), or within 30 days after you accept if no date was set.

We refund within 15 days of your notice. If we don’t, you can ask your credit card issuer to reverse the charge. Nothing in these terms limits these rights or the legal warranty on our work.

10. Your files
