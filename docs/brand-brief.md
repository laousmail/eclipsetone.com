## EclipseTone Brand Brief  
**Note for the coding/design agent:** This is a correction and refinement of the existing EclipseTone website build. The founder was not satisfied with the current version — this document should be treated as the authoritative source of truth going forward, superseding prior assumptions baked into the existing site.  
## 1. Executive snapshot  
EclipseTone Productions is the music production studio and artistic brand of Smail Laouchedi (artist name: Laousmail / styled SMA!L), based in Montréal, Québec. The brand covers two intertwined identities: Smail as a performing/recording artist, and EclipseTone as a service studio (recording, arranging, mixing, mastering, vocal production, sound design, and scoring for film/games). The site must center Smail as the artist first, while showcasing a growing team of collaborators. A defining current initiative is a public "15 songs before the end of 2026" challenge, building toward a real original-music stage show with a long-term dream of playing L'Olympia in Montréal. Core site goals: more bookings and more streams (follower count is explicitly not a priority — that's handled via organic social content).

## 3. Brand story & eclipse metaphor  
**Facts:**  
* The name's emotional seed came from witnessing the April 8, 2024 total solar eclipse in Montréal — his first time ever seeing one. He described the feeling as the name "singing" to him — "it's like a tone of the eclipse."  
* At the time, he was working a manual labor job. He went through a period of real difficulty — a job loss, personal struggle, and people leaving his life — a genuine low point.  
* Out of that low point came a sense of clarity: a feeling that he had to finally pursue what he wanted before it was too late, which he describes in spiritual/providential terms.  
* Shortly after deciding to start the studio, he received a MIDI keyboard found by a cousin, dusty and abandoned on the road — it still works perfectly today and has graffiti on it reading (paraphrased) "they don't care, just let them be" — which felt like a message to him. **Quote-worthy detail: keep this story intact, it's a strong brand anecdote.**  
* The meaning of "EclipseTone" has evolved: originally "me and EclipseTone" as a solo pursuit, now a growing team effort. He realized he couldn't do it all alone, needed to delegate to people better than him at specific things, and has come to see music as inherently collective — made in community, not just alone with a DAW.  
**Interpretation (for the agent, not a direct quote):** The eclipse is a personal symbol of a turning point — darkness giving way to a brief, intense, singular moment of light. That mirrors his own story: hitting bottom, then a sudden moment of clarity that changed his direction.  
## 4. Vision, mission, values  
**Timeline:**  
* **1 year:** More projects with talented artists; team operates without him needing to be present for everything; people doing what they love and getting paid.  
* **2 years:** Working worldwide/online, with stronger systems and foundations.  
* **5 years:** Working with brands and film — sound design, video/motion work, music for movies.  
* **10 years:** A full worldwide brand/label — "a big EclipseTone."  
**Non-negotiable values:**  
* No AI-generated music — both in his own production process and in not platforming/working with artists who rely on AI-generated music. Quote: *"We don't support that kind of shit."*  
* Won't work with disrespectful people or those lacking normal business etiquette.  
**Who this is for:**  
* Ideal client/artist: talented, sings well, has a good message and values aligned with his, and has the budget to invest.  
**Who this is NOT for:**  
* Artists who don't take music seriously, who treat it as disposable and ignore its craft and "divinity." Specifically calls out a subset of rappers who are careless about pitch/key and don't respect the work — not rap as a genre broadly, but that attitude.  
## 5. Current challenge (full detail)  
* **Name:** "15 songs before the end of 2026"  
* **Status:** 3 of 15 singles released so far, as of this writing.  
* **Origin:** He had a backlog of unreleased music. After connecting with artist Smail Hami (who encouraged him to release original music rather than only performing covers — they met after Smail performed covers at a bar show), he was motivated to put out original work and plan a real show together.  
* **Reference album:** Smail produced the album *Chants Kabyles Anciens* (note the plural "s" on both words) — this is the album connected to Smail Hami.  
* **Goal:** These 15 songs are meant to become the setlist for a real, fully-produced original live show — lighting, structure, a genuine production, not a bar gig. Long-term dream: playing L'Olympia in Montréal once he's built a large enough audience.  
* **Website feature request:** An admin-only control (accessible only to Smail) to add each new release as it drops — title + "click to listen" link — which automatically updates a public countdown/tracker (e.g., "4 of 15"). Unreleased song slots should appear as **mystery placeholders** — a teased, locked entry with a hint but no reveal, similar to a "secret admirer" feature on dating apps — to build anticipation.

## 11. Visual direction (black / red / orange / navy + usage rules)  
**Source of truth: the EclipseTone Productions logo itself** (provided as reference image) — the site's whole aesthetic should extend directly from it, not invent a new system.  
* **Base colors:** Black and white  
* **Accent:** A complex, non-pure orange — blended with red, yellow, and undertones of blue (not a flat/generic orange)  
* **Gradient motif:** The logo's eclipse glow transitions from warm gold-orange on one side to a cooler blue-grey on the other. This warm-to-cool gradient should be used throughout the site — in section transitions and backgrounds — as a recurring visual signature, not just confined to the logo.  
* **Typography:** Premium and highly legible. Should have a sense of physical depth — e.g., text that feels subtly lifted off the page via shadow treatment (soft drop shadows above/below text).  
* **Overall feel:** Minimalistic at first glance, but rewarding attention to detail on closer inspection — restrained, not flashy, but with intentional craft in every element.  
* **Imagery:** No existing photo/video library specified for this phase — use generated/placeholder imagery consistent with the brand mood for now (cinematic, eclipse/space-adjacent, premium) until real assets are supplied.  
* **Experience design:** The site should feel like scrolling through a story — not an obvious "our story" page, but a narrative that unfolds subtly as the user moves through it.

## 17. Implementation notes for the coding agent (explicit do / don't)  
**Do:**  
* Treat this document as a correction to the existing build — prior design decisions that conflict with this brief should be revised.  
* Pull the color and gradient system directly from the provided logo image (black/white base, warm-orange-to-cool-blue corona gradient), and carry that gradient into section transitions and backgrounds.  
* Build the challenge tracker as an admin-gated feature (visible only to Smail) that adds a title + listen link per release and auto-updates a public countdown; display unreleased slots as mystery/teaser placeholders.  
* Keep the site light on text; prioritize scroll-based visual storytelling.  
* Design typography with a premium feel and a subtle shadow-based depth effect.  
* Support bilingual (EN/FR) content, especially for the blog.  
* Make "Book a session" the persistent primary CTA.  
* Design for easy, frequent blog post additions (short-form, conversational).  
* Display pricing ranges for both song packages and film/motion scoring.  
**Don't:**  
* Don't invent or assume facts not stated in this document — anything unconfirmed is marked as TODO above and should be confirmed with Smail directly, not guessed.  
* Don't use a generic/flat orange — the accent color is a blended, complex orange per Section 11.  
* Don't build a long-form, text-heavy "about" experience — avoid walls of copy anywhere on the site.  
* Don't feature AI-generated music or messaging that could be read as endorsing AI-made music — this is a stated non-negotiable brand value.  
* Don't prioritize follower-count metrics or design around social growth — the site's job is bookings and streams.
